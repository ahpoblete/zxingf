## Summary

This app does not have a backend server, does not require an account, and does
not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Everything it
does happens on your device, with two narrow exceptions described below where
scanned barcode content is sent to Google's public lookup services to show you
more information about what you scanned.

## What This App Does (Barcode Scanner)

This app scans barcodes and QR codes using the device camera, and can also
generate barcodes from contacts, text, or other data you provide.

**Camera.** Used only to scan barcodes in real time. Camera frames are
processed on-device and are never stored or transmitted.

**Contacts (optional, user-initiated).** If you choose to share a contact as a
QR code, the app reads that one contact (via the system contact picker) to
encode it into a barcode image shown on your screen. Contact data is not
transmitted anywhere by the app itself.

**Wi-Fi.** If you scan a Wi-Fi network QR code, the app can configure your
device to join that network. This uses on-device Wi-Fi APIs only.

**Scan history.** Scanned codes are stored locally in a private database on
your device so you can review them later. You can clear this history at any
time in the app. If you choose to export/email your history or a generated
barcode image, that file is created in the app's private storage and handed
to whichever app you pick (e.g. your email client) — the developer never
receives it.

**Network lookups to Google (on by default, can be turned off).** When you
scan certain barcodes (e.g. book ISBNs or product/UPC codes), the app can
automatically look up the title, author, or price by sending the scanned
code to Google's public Book Search / Product Search services. If you use the
in-app "search inside this book" feature, your search text is also sent to
Google Books for that query. No other information (no contacts, no camera
images, no location, no device identifiers) is included in these requests.
You can disable this lookup entirely in the app's Settings → "Retrieve more
info."

**Opening links.** Some scan results (URLs, map addresses, app store links)
are opened in your browser, maps app, or the Play Store when you tap them.
That's standard link-opening — the destination site sees whatever a browser
visit normally sends, under its own privacy policy, not this app's.
